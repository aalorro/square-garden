## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Square Garden is a relaxing tile-swap puzzle game where you slide colorful tiles to form lines, squares, and shapes. With 126 handcrafted puzzle levels across 14 themed worlds — plus 4 unlockable challenge modes — this calm brain game has hours of offline play with no ads and no in-app purchases.

A perfect puzzle game for fans of match games, sliding puzzles, brain teasers, and color matching games — but with its own unique tile-swap twist.

GAMEPLAY — SIMPLE TO LEARN, DEEP TO MASTER
Swipe to swap any two adjacent tiles. Arrange same-colored tiles into patterns — lines, squares, L-shapes, T-shapes, and crosses — to complete each level's goals. Use fewer moves to earn up to 3 stars. Pull off a perfect game on World 5+ for 2x stars and bonus power-ups.

14 PUZZLE WORLDS TO EXPLORE
- Seedling Garden — Learn the basics on a cozy 5x5 board
- Blooming Meadow — Bigger 6x6 boards with multi-goal challenges
- Ancient Grove — Complex shapes on 7x7 boards
- Crystal Cavern — Frozen tiles that can't be moved but still count
- Shattered Isles — Void cells create irregular puzzle boards
- Void Fortress — Frozen tiles and voids combine on 8x8 grids
- Molten Core, Starfall Summit, Abyssal Depths, Prism Citadel — Master-level puzzles for serious puzzle solvers
- Nebula Verge, Quantum Lattice, Singularity Spire, Infinity Prism — Pro+-exclusive endgame worlds (levels 91–126) with the new Diagonal Movement power-up

4 CHALLENGE GAME MODES (World 5+)
Unlock special puzzle events through exceptional play. All challenges cost no lives and award bonus stars + power-up tokens.
- Blitz Garden — 60-second time attack puzzle with combo multipliers
- Overgrown Garden — Conquer a massive 9x9 puzzle board in 3 tries
- Shifting Sands — Tiles scramble every 3 swaps (2x stars)
- Memory Garden — Hidden tiles revealed only near your swaps (3x stars)

4 SKILL LEVELS — PLAY YOUR WAY
Choose your skill — and upgrade anytime in Settings:
- Casual — More moves, start at World 1, perfect for relaxing puzzle play
- Standard — Balanced puzzle challenge, start at World 2
- Pro — Fewer moves, start at World 3, for veteran puzzle solvers
- Pro+ — Tightest move budget, start at World 11 (level 91), unlocks four exclusive endgame worlds and the Diagonal Movement power-up

Each skill has unique rules for completed goals, from forgiving (Casual lets you swap through them) to strict (Pro and Pro+ lock them in). Skip the easy stuff if you're experienced — no grinding required.

5 TILE COLORS
Red, Blue, Yellow, Green — and Orange debuts in World 5 to keep the color matching fresh.

POWER-UPS
Power-up tiles appear on the board and are captured inside completed goals to earn tokens:
- Shuffle — Randomize the board when stuck
- Passthrough — Swap any two tiles, even across frozen ones
- Unfreeze — Free up locked tiles
- Redo — Free level restart
- Diagonal Movement (Pro+ / World 11+) — One swap with any of the 8 neighbours, even diagonally, skipping frozen tiles

REPLAYABILITY
- Randomized goal sets — every level has 4 different goal arrangements
- Three lives restore automatically after 3 consecutive wins
- Daily puzzle replay value with fresh challenges each time

CALMING AUDIO

- Calming sound effects for every swap, match, and win
